Rule or chatbot?

A group exercise for Week 1: write an if-rule, ask UvA AI Chat, and compare both with a human reading.

The question. Does the headline present the protest as violent? For each of the 20 headlines below the answer is yes or no.

Work in your group of four. About 20 minutes: 5 for the rule, 10 for the chatbot, 5 to compare. The headlines are made up for this exercise; some are in Dutch.

1Write a rule

The rule looks for each word anywhere in the headline, ignoring capitals. So riot also finds riots, and patriot.
