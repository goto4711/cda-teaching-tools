Pictures and words placed in one shared space. Train the space, then click a word or a picture to see its nearest neighbours.

Click a word or a picture.

Similarity is measured between the five-number vectors (cosine similarity, 1 = pointing the same way). The map is a flat sketch of those vectors, so distances on it are only approximate. Searching with a word returns the items at the top of this list: that is semantic search.
